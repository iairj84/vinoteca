Vinoteca 1.0

Wine cellar notebook with an AI sommelier.

Download the app

Android 8.0 or newer · 17.7 MB · debug build

  1. Tap the buttonThe APK downloads straight to your phone.
  2. Open the downloadTap the finished download (or find it in Files → Downloads) and confirm “Install”.
  3. Allow the installAndroid will ask you to allow installs from your browser. Settings → Install unknown apps → allow for this browser, then go back and tap Install again.
  4. Add your Groq keyOpen Vinoteca → Settings → paste a free key from console.groq.com → Test connection.

This is a debug-signed build, not from the Play Store — Google will warn you about that, and it is expected. It never touches your Google account and has no trackers. Your cellar, notes and mytaste.md stay on the phone; only photos you analyse are sent to Groq with your own key.
